## Quick Summary
**PrivateRank** is a decentralized, privacy-preserving gaming tournament platform built natively for the **Midnight Preprod Testnet** powered by **1AM Wallet**. 

- **ZK Proofs**: Verify player rank and tournament eligibility without revealing private personal data (Real Name, Email, Phone, Location).
- **Dual Tournament Modes**: Solo and Team tournaments with automated team size & capacity calculations.
- **Strict Role Separation**: Distinct Organizer and Player identity models with multi-layer authorization guards.
- **On-Chain Transactions**: Real wallet signing via 1AM Wallet and transaction lifecycle management on Midnight Preprod.
